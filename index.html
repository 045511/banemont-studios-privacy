1. Who we are and what this policy covers

BANEMONT STUDIOS (“we”, “us” or “our”) develops and publishes games. Our app-store publisher name may appear as BANEMONT. This Privacy Policy covers our games and related player services that link to this policy, together with the game notices listed below.

Each game notice explains the features, providers and data practices that apply to that game. A feature described for one game does not mean every BANEMONT STUDIOS game has that feature. An additional notice or in-game explanation may provide more specific information. If there is a difference, the more specific notice applies to that feature.

Currently covered game: Astro Exit

Android package com.astroexit.game. Local puzzle play, optional Worldwide pilots and Challenge rankings, and rewarded advertising where enabled.

Read the Astro Exit privacy notice →

Privacy and support questions can be sent to info@banemont.org. Please name the game in your request.

2. Information we use

The information used depends on the game, the installed version and the features you choose. The current game notices identify the applicable categories.

Local game information

A game may keep progress, checkpoints, settings, earned currency, cosmetic selections, power ups and local scores on your device. These records enable saved play and preferences. An operating system or a device backup service may separately back up or restore app data under its own settings.

Information you choose to provide

If you use an online feature, the applicable game may receive a chosen player name, selected flag or region, and the information needed to maintain that online identity. A support request includes the email address, message and attachments you send us. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Online gameplay and technical information

Online services can receive account or installation identifiers, authentication credentials, scores, game and map versions, actions and timing needed to validate a run, request identifiers, and security or diagnostic information. Network providers receive connection information, such as an IP address, when processing requests. An anonymous or randomly assigned identifier can still distinguish a player or installation.

Advertising information

Where an advertising SDK is enabled, its provider may process advertising or other device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The advertising section and game notice explain the provider and available controls.

3. Why information is used

We use the information associated with the features you use to:

  • Run the game, save local progress and apply your preferences.
  • Create, authenticate or recover an optional online player identity.
  • Validate online results, operate rankings, prevent duplicate submissions and investigate unfair or abusive activity.
  • Deliver optional rewarded advertising and verify that a reward was earned before crediting it.
  • Operate and secure our services, diagnose failures, control request volume and prevent fraud.
  • Answer support or privacy requests, verify ownership when necessary and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the activity: providing a feature you request, consent where required, permitted legitimate interests in operating and securing our services, or a legal obligation. We seek required consent through the relevant feature or privacy controls; this policy does not itself grant consent to every type of processing.

4. Who receives information

Service providers

Information is processed by providers used to host and secure a game, authenticate online players, deliver advertising, distribute the app or handle email support. The current providers and their roles are identified in each game notice. Providers may also handle information for their own purposes under their privacy policies and applicable law.

Other players

If you submit to a public leaderboard, the fields described in the game notice are visible to other people. Use a player name that does not disclose your real name, email address or other private details.

Legal and security disclosures

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal process, protect players and services, investigate fraud or security incidents, or establish and defend legal claims. A business transfer could involve relevant records, subject to applicable safeguards and any required notice.

Advertising-related sharing

Advertising providers receive the information described in the advertising notice. Depending on the law that applies and how ads are configured, some advertising-related disclosures may be treated as a “sale”, “sharing” or targeted advertising. We do not describe advertising-enabled games as collecting or sharing no data. Use the available privacy choices or contact us about rights that apply to you.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Astro Exit uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform in Android builds where ads are enabled. Google’s SDK processes data for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. See the Astro Exit notice for its behavior.

Rewarded-ad viewing is optional. However, an enabled SDK may initialize or prepare ads before you tap a Watch Ad button when the privacy requirements checked by the consent service permit requests. Test ads also use a real SDK and are not a promise of no data processing.

Review the advertising privacy options presented in the game, where available, and your device’s advertising controls. Android may let you reset or delete an advertising identifier. Withdrawing consent affects future processing that relies on that consent; it does not automatically delete previously held information. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve technical processing.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Android SDK data disclosure.

6. How long information is kept

Retention depends on the type of information and why it is needed.

  • Local records stay on your device until changed, reset or removed. Device backups can separately preserve copies.
  • Online player and ranking records are kept to provide the online feature and related validation or recovery, subject to a verified deletion request and any permitted retention described in the game notice.
  • Operational and security records are kept as needed to operate services, investigate problems, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Some request counters are pruned automatically.
  • Support correspondence is kept for handling the request and related follow-up, and where needed for security, legal or recordkeeping purposes.
  • Provider logs and backups follow the provider’s retention and recovery processes. Deleting active records does not instantly erase every historical backup or log.

When handling a deletion request, we may need to retain limited information for legal, security or confirmation purposes. The Astro Exit notice explains its minimal deletion-confirmation record. Contact us about retention or the status of a request; we do not promise a single retention period for every provider and category.

7. Security and international processing

We use measures appropriate to the information and service, including HTTPS for online service requests, authenticated access and restrictions on private records. Keep private recovery keys, session credentials and deletion receipts secure. Do not post them publicly or send them in an ordinary support email.

Providers can process or store information outside your province, state or country. Privacy protections and lawful access rules may differ in those places. Applicable legal requirements and provider safeguards govern those transfers. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can use the settings and choices offered in the relevant game, choose a non-identifying player name, decline optional ad viewing, and request deletion of an online identity.

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; or opt out of qualifying sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Some rights are subject to exceptions and identity verification. We will not penalize you for exercising rights where the law prohibits doing so.

Send a request to info@banemont.org and identify the game. We may ask for the minimum additional information needed to verify that the request concerns your account. A public player name alone does not establish ownership. We respond in accordance with applicable requirements and explain if we cannot fulfill all or part of a request.

You may also raise a concern with the privacy regulator or supervisory authority relevant to where you live. Where an authorized agent may act for you, we may need evidence of authorization.

Request account or data deletion

9. Account and data deletion

You can start a deletion request without reinstalling a game through our account and data deletion page, which provides an email request pathway. If an in-game deletion control is available in your version, you can use that too.

Submitting a request is not confirmation that deletion is complete. Ownership may need to be verified, and removal may take processing time. We will confirm the outcome or explain any additional steps. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, recovery keys or deletion receipts.

Deleting an online player identity and deleting local game data are separate actions. Uninstalling or clearing the app does not by itself request server-account deletion. Removing a server identity does not automatically erase campaign progress or currency saved on a device. The game notice describes what is in scope.

10. Children and age suitability

Astro Exit is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. A stated intended audience is not age verification. The game’s store information and game notice describe its audience; other games must provide their own notice if their audience or practices differ.

We do not knowingly seek personal information from children in a way that violates applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact info@banemont.org. We will investigate and take the steps required by applicable law, including deletion where appropriate.

11. This privacy website and external links

These privacy pages are designed for GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or browser-storage features to them. The pages do not contain a form that collects a recovery key or password.

GitHub states that visits to GitHub Pages involve logging and storing visitor IP addresses for security, even when a visitor is not signed in. GitHub handles that hosting information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Selecting an email link opens your email application; your email provider and ours process the resulting message.

External links lead to services with their own privacy practices. This policy describes BANEMONT STUDIOS practices and does not replace those services’ notices.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy and the relevant game notices when practices change, including when a new game or provider is added. The revision date appears at the top. Where a material change requires additional notice or consent, we provide it as required. An updated policy is not a substitute for any consent that the law requires.
