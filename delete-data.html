Start with one email

  • Contact info@banemont.org and identify the game.
  • Include your public pilot name if known; ownership still needs verification.
  • Never email a recovery key, password, session token or private deletion receipt.
  • We confirm the outcome after verification and processing; a request is not instant deletion.
  • Online-account deletion and removal of local device saves are separate.

1. Start a request by email

BANEMONT STUDIOS · published as BANEMONT

info@banemont.org

Suggested subject: Account or data deletion request — [game name].

Email an Astro Exit deletion request

If the button does not open an email application, copy the address into your email service. This page does not send a request automatically.

You can initiate a request without having the game installed. Please include:

  • The game name. For Astro Exit, the Android package is com.astroexit.game.
  • Your public player or pilot name and selected flag, if known.
  • Whether you want the online account and associated data deleted, or have another privacy request.
  • Whether you still have access to the original installation or an available recovery method. Do not include the recovery key itself.

You may also request deletion of selected data while keeping your account. Identify what you want removed. We will verify ownership and explain any information needed to continue the account, or retained for legitimate reasons.

Keep credentials private. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, private recovery keys or deletion receipts. A public pilot name alone is not proof that you own the account.

2. Verification and confirmation

We review the request and explain any additional steps needed to verify ownership securely. Provide only the information requested for that process. If you cannot access the original device, tell us so we can discuss the available options.

Sending an email or using an in-game control starts the request; it does not prove that deletion has completed. We will confirm the outcome or explain any steps, limits or permitted retention that apply. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law; this page does not promise an automatic or instant response.

If your installed version has an in-game deletion control, you may also use it. For Astro Exit, look in Challenge → Pilot. If the control is missing or reports an unavailable service, the email request pathway remains available.

3. What Astro Exit deletion covers

After verified account deletion is completed, the scope includes the active online pilot, Worldwide scores, stored Challenge runs and replay records, recovery record, account-linked request counters and backing anonymous authentication account.

It does not automatically remove campaign progress, coins, cosmetics, power ups, ad-reward receipts or device-only scores saved on your device. You can manage local data through the game’s available controls or Android app-storage settings. Clearing app storage can permanently remove local progress; uninstalling the app does not itself request online-account deletion.

For another game linked to the studio policy, identify that game in your request so we can explain its account and local-data scope.

4. Limited retained information

Limited information may be retained where needed for security, fraud prevention, applicable legal obligations or confirmation of an interrupted deletion. For receipt-based Astro Exit deletion, a minimal record may retain a cryptographic receipt hash, completion state and operational timestamps or attempt metadata; completed records no longer contain the deleted pilot’s name, flag, authentication-account identifier, scores or replays. Those confirmation records currently have no automatic expiry.

Support messages and provider logs or backups are subject to the retention practices described in the studio policy and Astro Exit notice. Removal from active service records is not an immediate erasure of every provider backup or historical log. We explain any relevant retention when responding to your request.

5. How this page handles information

This is a static page. It has no account sign-in, recovery-key form, tracking scripts, analytics or browser-storage features added by BANEMONT STUDIOS. GitHub Pages processes ordinary connection information, including IP addresses, for hosting security. Clicking an email link opens your chosen email application; the email is processed by your email provider and ours when you send it.
